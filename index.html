Research Projects

Neural Network Models for Visual Working Memory

• July 2025 - Present

Under the guidance of Prof. Rakesh Sengupta, I am trying to understand the variation of the self-excitation parameter in the On-Centre Off-Surround model widely used to understand Visual Working Memory. I presented this work at the Advanced Network Technologies and Intelligence Computing (ANTIC-2025) conference, and won a Young Scientist DST Grant for the same.

Embodied Cognition in Tamil Bilingual Speakers

• January 2025 - Present

As a research assistant in this study, I have been working to understand the correlation between early learned verbs and body parts and generational differences in Tamil-English bilingual speakers under the guidance of Prof. Madhavilatha Maganti, along with Dr. Nitya Sethuraman from the University of Michigan-Dearborn and Dr. Josita Maouene from Grand Valley State University, USA. I have collected qualitative data from around 25 participants till now for the same.
